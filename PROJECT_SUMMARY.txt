🔬 NOVEL FEATURES (PATENT-WORTHY)

The system engineers 6 innovative fraud signals and combines them into a
unified "multi-signal risk embedding vector":

1. 📊 Balance Discrepancy Ratio
   └─ Detects mathematical inconsistencies in account balance changes
   └─ Formula: (expected_new_balance - actual_new_balance) / amount
   └─ Fraud indicator: Accounting manipulations

2. 📈 Transaction Velocity Score
   └─ Counts transactions from same origin in rolling window
   └─ Fraud indicator: Rapid-fire attack patterns

3. 📉 Amount Deviation Score
   └─ Measures deviation from sender's historical mean (Z-score)
   └─ Fraud indicator: Abnormal transaction sizes

4. 🔒 Zero Balance Flag
   └─ Binary: Did origin account hit zero after transaction?
   └─ Fraud indicator: Account draining

5. 💰 Round Amount Suspicion
   └─ Flags suspiciously round amounts (1000, 5000, etc.)
   └─ Fraud indicator: Unnatural transaction patterns

6. 🕐 Destination Account Age Proxy
   └─ Estimates account maturity from naming patterns
   └─ Fraud indicator: Targeting new, vulnerable accounts

🤖 DUAL-MODEL ENSEMBLE ARCHITECTURE

Model 1: Isolation Forest
  • Tree-based unsupervised anomaly detection
  • Isolates anomalies through random partitioning
  • Excellent for high-dimensional outlier detection
  • Fast, interpretable, robust

Model 2: Autoencoder (PyTorch)
  • Deep neural network reconstruction model
  • Learns normal transaction patterns
  • Flags high reconstruction errors as anomalies
  • Captures complex non-linear patterns

Ensemble Combination
  • Weighted average: 0.5 × IF_score + 0.5 × AE_score
  • Leverages complementary strengths
  • More robust than single model
  • Final score: 0-1 range (0=normal, 1=anomalous)
